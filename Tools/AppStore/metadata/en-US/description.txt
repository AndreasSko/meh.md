The note-taking app of my dreams - that was the goal of this app.
Markdown first, minimalistic, and having a great sync. It’s not meant as an Obsidian replacement, but borrows some of its (and other plugins') features that were most useful to me, including:
- Pretty nice iCloud* sync: You can edit the same note on multiple devices, and don't need to worry about conflicts
- Snippets and templates
- Version history (which gives me peace of mind when rewriting notes)
- Widgets, so you can open pinned and recent notes directly from your Home Screen
- (Back)links
- iPhone, iPad, and Mac support
- ..and: no need for yet another account or subscription!

But why is it called meh? Well.. First of all I found it funny. It also sounded like “my” (because it’s MY Markdown app), and it should be a bit.. Meh. Nothing too fancy; an app that Just. Works.

Maybe it works for you too? :)

*there is also support for S3-compatible storage for the nerds, but it is still “in beta”
