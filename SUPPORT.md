# Support

I build meh.md mainly for myself, as a notes app that works the way I
want. If it's useful for you too, great!

If you find a bug, feel free to send me a report and I'll try to fix it:

- **In the app:** open Settings and tap **Send Feedback**. If your email
  app allows it, this attaches the sync event log, which helps me find
  sync problems. It contains no note contents or names.
- **Email:** [meh@andreas-sk.de](mailto:meh@andreas-sk.de)
- **GitHub:** [open an issue](https://github.com/AndreasSko/meh.md/issues)

Please mention your device and what you did before the problem
appeared.

Feature ideas are welcome too, but please don't expect an answer. I
work on the features I need or find interesting.
